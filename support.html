Report a problem

Tell us your device model, iOS version, game version and what happened. Screenshots can help. Please do not send passwords, full payment card details or other sensitive information.

Restore purchases

Use the game's Restore Purchases option while signed into the Apple Account used for your original purchase. If a purchase does not appear, contact us with the product name and a description of the issue.

Refund requests

Apple processes in-app payments. You can request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem service; eligibility is determined by Apple.

Rewarded ads

Rewards are delivered when the advertising SDK confirms completion and the ad closes. If an ad fails to load, check your connection and try again. Contact us if a completed ad did not deliver the expected reward.
