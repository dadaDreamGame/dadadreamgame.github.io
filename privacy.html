Battle of Snake

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Battle of Snake is operated by HUIDA ZHANG under the brand Dada Dream Games. Contact: dada1029@gmail.com. This policy describes the game and its support website.

1. Game data on your device

The game stores settings, player names, scores, progress, virtual currency balances, unlocks, rewarded-ad completion counts and custom skin data locally on your device. The current game does not require a developer-operated login account or upload this progress to our own server. Device backups may include local game data according to your Apple settings.

2. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide rewarded advertising. According to Google's SDK disclosures, advertising services may collect IP addresses (which can be used to estimate general location), device or account identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic and performance information. These services use data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve their services. Personalization and identifier availability depend on applicable consent, device settings and the app's configuration.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information and Google's iOS SDK data disclosures.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The game uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information, such as product identifiers and purchase status, to validate purchases and restore access. We do not receive your full payment card or bank account details. Apple's handling of payment and account data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate issues and handle privacy requests. Email is processed through our email provider, Google. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your request.

5. Website visits

The pages themselves do not include advertising, analytics scripts or forms. The selected hosting provider may process IP addresses and technical request logs to deliver and secure the website. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to third-party websites are governed by those websites' policies.

6. Choices and requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, or to raise an objection or other privacy request available under applicable law. Consent-based processing may be withdrawn where applicable. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Local game data can generally be removed by deleting the app and, if applicable, managing device backups. This does not erase Apple's purchase records or data independently held by advertising services.

Advertising choices may also be available through device settings and Google's privacy tools. Any in-game consent and privacy controls provided in your release allow you to review the choices available for your region.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Where relevant data-protection laws apply, we process support information to respond to requests, provide assistance and maintain service security based on applicable contractual obligations or legitimate interests. Processing requiring consent, including certain advertising activities, is subject to that consent. Information may also be processed to meet legal obligations.

8. Retention, sharing and international processing

Local game data remains on your device until changed or removed. We retain support communications for as long as reasonably needed to resolve requests, maintain relevant records and meet legal obligations, and review them for deletion when no longer needed. Apple, Google and the hosting provider apply their own retention practices. Service providers may process data in countries outside your country of residence, subject to applicable safeguards. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support communications. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Advertising must be configured to reflect the game's intended audience and applicable age-related requirements.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The revised date will appear at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated as required.
